THE DETAILS THAT MAKE OR BREAK A TRIP

Five things to check before you commit.

01Exact location

Check the distance to the places you want to visit, river crossings, last-mile transport and night-time access.

02Recent reviews

Read the newest critical reviews as well as positive ones. Look for repeated comments about cleanliness, food, staff and noise.

03Full price

Confirm taxes, meals, parking, activity fees, deposits, extra-person charges and transport add-ons.

04Season & safety

Check rain, trail closures, tides, forest permissions, water conditions and operator safety equipment.

05Booking terms

Ask for written confirmation, cancellation rules, check-in times and the property’s direct contact details.
