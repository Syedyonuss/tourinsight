# TourInsight 3.5 — Trust-first discovery

## What's new
- Compare up to three stays or activities side by side.
- See source date, area, reported price context, traveller signal and practical caveats together.
- Open independent recent-review searches, Maps location checks and Tripadvisor searches for each candidate.
- Filter by a reported amount ceiling. Unknown-price listings remain visible; traveller figures may represent different units and must not be compared without checking the unit.
- No fake star ratings, fake review counts, or algorithmic "best hotel" claims.

## What is not live yet
- No live hotel availability or rate feed is connected.
- Review search links open external providers; TourInsight does not scrape or republish their ratings.
- Reddit discussions are curated source links, not a continuously refreshed Reddit feed.
